Good to know

Before you start growing.

Can I play MazeBloom offline?

Yes. The complete Campaign, Daily Bloom selection, progress, and puzzle rules work locally. Endless Garden generation also runs on your device. Advertising features in an ad-supported build need a network, but ordinary gameplay remains available when ads are not.

Is Endless Garden truly infinite?

MazeBloom calls it practically unbounded, not mathematically infinite. After its certified 100-level starter, it generates and certifies puzzles as you progress while checking against the history retained on your device.

What happens if I clear the app's data?

Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress, settings, Coins, Companion ownership/selection/ad credit, replays, and Endless Garden history. Because backup and device transfer are disabled, that history is not restored automatically.

Does MazeBloom require an account?

No. MazeBloom does not require a player account. See the privacy policy for details about local data and the optional Google advertising services used by an ad-supported release.
