# Monetization, consent, and analytics

Gameplay remains configuration-free and offline. `AdsGateway`, `BillingGateway`, `ConsentGateway`, and `Analytics` isolate optional services. Debug builds integrate Google Mobile Ads 25.4 and UMP 4.0 with Google’s published test app, interstitial, and rewarded IDs. Automated policy tests retain deterministic fake gateways. The offline `release` build excludes both SDK dependencies and binds the safe no-op adapter. The separate `production` build includes the real adapter and enables it only when all three externally supplied IDs are syntactically valid and are not the checked-in Google test IDs. No live ID, billing product, or credential is checked in.

The first completion of any Campaign, Daily, or Auto Progressive level credits 10 Coins in the same Room transaction as progress. Replays do not pay again. A hint atomically spends 30 Coins when the balance is sufficient; otherwise the Hint button shows an ad icon and one verified rewarded-ad transaction authorizes that hint without altering the remaining balance. Skip always shows an ad icon; one verified rewarded ad advances to the next eligible Campaign or Progressive level without awarding completion Coins. Consumed transaction IDs are persisted so duplicate callbacks cannot grant an action twice.

Every fifth first completion transaction marks one pending interstitial. It is consumed when the player leaves the completion result through Next or Back, never during a move, failure, Undo, Restart, or tutorial cue. Debug device builds exercise the real SDK callback path with visible Google test ads. The debug-only Developer garden can present either format without changing Coins, progress, or cadence. In the offline release, unavailable ads fail safely: hints remain unavailable without enough Coins and Skip does not advance.

Typed analytics cover app open, onboarding, level/daily lifecycle, Undo/Restart/hints, replay/share, generator errors, ads, and purchases. Allowed parameters are stable IDs and coarse gameplay metadata. Names, contacts, media, precise location, raw boards/solutions, and unconsented advertising identifiers are prohibited.

To ship live ads, supply approved production AdMob identifiers through `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_APP_ID`, `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_INTERSTITIAL_ID`, and `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_REWARDED_ID`; complete consent/privacy disclosures and Play Data Safety declarations; and validate production inventory on signed internal-test builds. Those external approvals and identifiers are not needed to build or exercise the official test inventory locally.
