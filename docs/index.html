MazeBloom Privacy Policy

MazeBloom is designed as an offline puzzle game. Your puzzles, progress, settings, and generated Endless Garden history stay on your device. An ad-supported build may use Google services only as described below.

1. Overview

MazeBloom does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or camera. The developer does not operate an analytics server for the app.

The Play Store may offer either an offline build without advertising libraries or an ad-supported build that uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The store listing identifies whether the installed version contains ads.

2. Data stored on your device

MazeBloom stores only the information needed to run the game locally:

Game progressCompleted and skipped levels, stars, best move counts, replays, unlocked content, coin balance, daily streaks, selected and permanently unlocked Animal Companions, per-Companion rewarded-ad credit, and an unfinished attempt.
PreferencesTheme, sound, haptics, reduced motion, high contrast, and direction-control settings.
Endless Garden historyGenerated puzzle definitions, certification records, fingerprints, and local history used to avoid repeats.
Daily rewards and ad controlsThe last local day credited with daily Coins, random rewarded-ad callback identifiers, the last completed rewarded-ad time, and the last confirmed App Open impression time. These values prevent duplicate rewards and enforce ad timing protections.

This data is stored in the app's private Android storage. It is not uploaded to the developer. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for MazeBloom so that its local puzzle history is not copied by the app's backup configuration.

3. Optional advertising and consent services

The offline release does not package the Google Mobile Ads or UMP libraries and does not request internet, network-state, advertising-ID, or Privacy Sandbox ad permissions.

If the ad-supported production build is installed, it uses Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.4.0 and UMP SDK 4.0.0. UMP refreshes consent information before the ads SDK is initialized or an ad is requested. Where required, UMP displays a consent message and a privacy-options entry is available in MazeBloom Settings.

This build may request an App Open ad when MazeBloom enters the foreground, with at least one hour between confirmed App Open impressions. A rewarded ad is requested only when you choose an insufficient-Coin Hint, Undo, or Skip action, or explicitly choose to apply 50 Coins of credit to one locked Animal Companion. Companion ad credit stays on your device, applies only to that chosen animal, and does not increase your general Coin balance. A standard interstitial is attempted after every fifth first-time level completion, but is suppressed for 60 seconds after a rewarded ad is completed. Ads that are unavailable or cannot be shown do not block normal gameplay or apply a reward.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • user product interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic information such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.

Google says this data is encrypted in transit. Google and participating advertising partners process advertising data under their own terms and policies. MazeBloom does not add developer-operated analytics or sell player data.

Learn more: Google Mobile Ads data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information for advertising.

4. Sharing a result

When you choose Share, MazeBloom captures the visible celebration screen, writes a PNG image to its cache, and opens Android's system share sheet with that image and the MazeBloom Play Store URL. Nothing is shared until you select another app or destination. The selected service receives the image and share text under that service's own privacy policy. MazeBloom does not receive the recipient list or a record of where you shared it.

5. App permissions

The offline release requires no sensitive Android permissions. It grants temporary, read-only access to a generated share image only to the app you select in the Android share sheet.

The ad-supported build may declare internet and network-state access plus advertising-related permissions supplied by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. MazeBloom does not request contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone, SMS, or precise-location permission.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear MazeBloom's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Cached share images may be removed by Android automatically and are deleted with app storage. Clearing storage or uninstalling also erases Endless Garden uniqueness history, so previously generated puzzles may appear again after a fresh install.

Because MazeBloom has no developer account or cloud profile, there is no server-side player account to delete. For an ad-supported build, Google and its partners control retention of data they process; use the privacy options in MazeBloom Settings, Android's advertising controls, and the links above for applicable choices or deletion requests.

7. Security

MazeBloom stores gameplay data in app-private storage, disables app backup and device transfer, blocks cleartext network traffic, and uses no developer-operated data backend. Google states that Mobile Ads data is protected in transit using TLS. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free, but the app limits collection and network access by design.

8. Children

MazeBloom is not directed to children under 13, and the current ad-supported implementation is not configured as a mixed-audience or child-directed service. The app does not knowingly ask children for personal information. If a future release is offered to age groups that include children, its advertising and consent configuration, store declarations, and this policy must be updated before publication.

9. Your choices

  • Play the offline release without an account or developer-operated network service.
  • Decline or adjust advertising choices when UMP presents them, where available.
  • Open Settings → Privacy and purchases → Privacy options when the ad-supported build says the control is required.
  • Reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device's privacy settings.
  • Do not use Share, or cancel the Android share sheet, if you do not want to send a result to another service.
  • Delete all MazeBloom local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when MazeBloom's features, vendors, or legal obligations change. Material changes will be reflected by a new “Last updated” date and, when appropriate, an in-app or store-listing notice. The current policy applies to the current release.
