# Monetization, consent, and analytics

Gameplay remains configuration-free and offline. `AdsGateway`, `BillingGateway`, `ConsentGateway`, and `Analytics` isolate optional services. Debug builds integrate Google Mobile Ads 25.4 and UMP 4.0 with Google's published test app, App Open, interstitial, and rewarded IDs. Automated policy tests retain deterministic fake gateways. The offline `release` build excludes both SDK dependencies and binds the safe no-op adapter. The separate `production` build includes the real adapter and enables it only when all four externally supplied IDs are syntactically valid and are not the checked-in Google test IDs. No live ID, billing product, credential, or production analytics backend is checked in.

The app credits 30 daily Coins only when the observed local date moves forward. The first completion of any Campaign, Daily, or Endless level credits 10 Coins in the same Room transaction as progress; replays do not pay again. Hint and Undo atomically spend 30 Coins, and Skip Level atomically spends 50 Coins, when the balance is sufficient. Below the applicable threshold, the button shows an ad affordance and one Google Mobile Ads reward callback authorizes that action without altering the Coin balance. Consumed transaction IDs and their action labels are persisted so duplicate callbacks cannot authorize an action twice. Unavailable or incomplete rewarded ads leave both the action and Coin balance unchanged.

Every fifth first-completion transaction marks one pending standard interstitial. The controller consumes and attempts it immediately after solved-board persistence. A completed rewarded ad suppresses standard interstitial presentation for 60 seconds; the due attempt is not delayed into an unrelated screen. App Open ads are requested on foreground entry and have a persisted two-hour cooldown measured from a confirmed impression. One full-screen presentation mutex prevents App Open, interstitial, and rewarded formats from overlapping, and ads fail open for gameplay.

Debug device builds exercise the real SDK callback path with visible Google test ads. The debug-only Developer garden can present standard-interstitial or rewarded test inventory without changing Coins, progress, or cadence; App Open test inventory is requested through the normal foreground lifecycle. The offline release cannot show ads, so insufficient-balance actions remain unavailable instead of being granted for free.

Typed analytics cover app open, onboarding, level/daily lifecycle, Undo/Restart/hints, replay/share, generator errors, ad requests/results, and purchase placeholders. Debug writes these events to Logcat; non-debug builds currently bind `NoOpAnalytics`. Allowed parameters are stable IDs and coarse gameplay metadata. Names, contacts, media, precise location, raw boards/solutions, and unconsented advertising identifiers are prohibited.

To ship live ads, supply approved production AdMob identifiers through `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_APP_ID`, `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_APP_OPEN_ID`, `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_INTERSTITIAL_ID`, and `MAZEBLOOM_ADMOB_REWARDED_ID`; complete consent/privacy disclosures and Play Data Safety declarations; and validate production inventory on signed internal-test builds. Those external approvals and identifiers are not needed to build or exercise Google's official test inventory locally.
